Computers used to be human beings who computed equations and such for corporations

Then computers became a tool used by human beings to create value

Now computers are becoming a tool used by themselves to create value with human oversight

Soon, computers will be running themselves to create value (no human needed)

What are humans supposed to do then to make good on their existence?
